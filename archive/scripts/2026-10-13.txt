## Anthropic's distillation defenses
A: Anthropic's other paper is the mirror image. Instead of a model escaping, it's a model's capabilities being copied out through the front door. Distillation attacks, in Anthropic's words, covert, industrial-scale campaigns to extract one model's capabilities into another.

A: We did, and here's why that pays off today. The lesson was that an A-P-I hands you sampled text, not the model's full probabilities, so the practical way to copy a reasoning model is to harvest its reasoning traces. And Anthropic now says exactly that. The most valuable kind of data for this purpose is transcripts of Claude's reasoning, the thinking process Claude writes out before it responds.
B: So the thinking is the crown jewels.
A: Apparently. [chuckles] Which is an odd way to find out what you're worth. They describe three defenses. The first is controlling access to that reasoning.
