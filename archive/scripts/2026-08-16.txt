## Who is actually in the workspace
A: The reason this paper exists is that almost everything we know about firms adopting A I comes from surveys. You ask people whether they use it. People are bad at that — they misremember, they round up, they answer the question they think you are asking. This is the other kind of evidence: administrative records from inside the product.
B: Which has its own disease.
A: Which has a very specific disease, and I want it on the table before the numbers rather than after. This is OpenAI measuring OpenAI. It sees ChatGPT Enterprise and nothing else — not the A P I, not personal accounts, not a competitor's model, not the thing somebody's platform team built in-house. So every sentence that follows describes activity inside one product's walls.
B: Fine. Inside the walls.
A: The worker-level sample is one thousand seven hundred sixty-four organizations and about seventeen and a half million messages. There is a smaller slice with the task classifier attached — nine hundred seventy-three organizations, roughly eight point seven million classified messages. Everything they measure about workers is at a fixed point: twenty-six weeks, six months, after that organization adopted.
