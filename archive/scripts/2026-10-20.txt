## Don't trust the model you sell
B: So. The insider threat. Let me start with what the phrase means in security, because it's an old, specific idea. Most defenses face outward. Firewalls, logins, the locked front door. An insider threat program is for the person already inside, with a badge and legitimate access. You can't lock them out, because they're supposed to be there. So you watch what they do, and you flag anything outside their role. The accountant who suddenly downloads the engineering repository.
A: And Microsoft's claim is that an agent is that accountant.
B: Exactly that. Nadella laid it out in a post on X, titled "Models as Insider Risks in the Super Intelligence Era." A: In the nineteen-forties and fifties, people used the phrase "automatic programming." First it meant automating the punching of paper tape.
