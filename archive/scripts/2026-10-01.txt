## The decision-model gold rush
A: Quick refresher, because we taught this one properly in a deep dive. A decision model doesn't write you an answer. You give it a situation and a typed question, yes or no, pick one of these, rate on this scale. It does one pass through the network and reads probabilities straight off the end. No tokens generated. Jev, from TypeSafe, was the first to sell it as a product.
B: And now everybody has one.
A: Within about two weeks. Start with OpenAI, since it's the biggest name. Sam Altman described the Decisions API as giving their Luna model "a predefined set of options to choose between," and said, "By focusing the model on that choice, we can make it extremely fast." On the Latent Space podcast, OpenAI's Nikunj Handa was strikingly candid. It's Luna's existing weights. Reasoning switched off. Priced the same as Luna. It takes images, which Latent Space notes Jev doesn't. And, his words, "This whole thing started, like, a week ago."
B: [chuckles] A week.
A: TypeSafe's chief executive, Diogo Almeida, joked on X about "the beginning of the clone wars," and then said, "Intelligence is the hard part."
