## Watermarking from the implementer's chair
B: We covered the mechanism on the fourteenth — the key sorting viable next words into groups, the tournament that keeps the odds honest — and I'm not re-teaching it. A: That's a genuinely important distinction and I want to say why. If it were in the weights, an open-weight release would carry the mark and you could study it. Because it's in the sampling code, it lives on the serving path.
